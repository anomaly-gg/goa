📋 Overnight rules

Getting overnight

  • You need the owner's approval. No walk-in overnight after 10 PM.
  • You can request from 6 AM to 10 PM, for tonight only.
  • Your approval is good for one night. Request again for the next night.

During curfew (10 PM – 4 AM)

  • If you log out, your overnight ends. You can't log back in until curfew is over.
  • Switching PCs? Log back in within 5 minutes and it doesn't count.
  • You can lock your seat. That's fine.
  • If you come back after leaving, you get a warning. 2 warnings in 30 days = no overnight for 7 days.
  • Emergency? Message the owner before you step out. If the owner says OK, it won't count.
